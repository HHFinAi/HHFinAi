## Deliberately deferred

A large adaptation-company dataset, additional standalone agents, a complex dashboard and broad jurisdictional rule coverage remain deferred until core evidence and review are stronger. A small, defensible public-evidence dataset is preferable to an unverified reproduction of a bank universe.

## Methodological readings

The development direction draws on Nuveen's AI investor guide; UBS's physical-risk FAQs and September 2026 ESG news review; Barclays' adaptation-solutions study; and J.P. The [case source register](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/blob/main/examples/microsoft-ai/SOURCES.md) identifies each contribution and its limits.
