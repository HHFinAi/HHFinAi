## Implementation boundaries

Several repositories share a versioned, independently vendored workflow core. Multiple repository names do not mean multiple independently developed AI models. Most local engines do not include an LLM, live data feed, scheduler or broker connection. Synthetic examples exercise workflow behavior; methodology-only studies can intentionally stop at `NEEDS_DATA`.

Current law, market inputs and issuer facts require separately dated verification. A workflow title is not certification of regulatory compliance, investment accuracy or production security. Read the relevant source, validation and control documentation before use.
