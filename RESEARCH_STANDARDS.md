# Research standards and AI-use boundaries

[Selected research](README.md) · [Development roadmap](RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md)

These standards describe the portfolio's intended research process. Case-specific evidence and executed checks establish what was actually done; this document is not an audit opinion or a substitute for missing validation.

## Evidence and claim types

Keep **reported facts**, **management expectations**, **analyst assumptions**, **calculations**, **inferences** and **unknowns** distinct. Register the source, date, entity, financial period, unit, population and relevant document location. An issuer statement can establish what management says without independently proving implementation or effectiveness.

Do not relabel group capex as AI-only capex, water withdrawals as consumption, employee-plus-customer training as workforce outcomes, or proposed spending as completed assets. A correct number with the wrong denominator is not a correct investment input.

Conflicting passages remain visible until reconciled. A source citation does not by itself prove textual support. The Microsoft case includes examples of attribution and denominator challenges from the methodological readings; it does not silently replace them with alternative estimates.

## Financial analysis

Specify the counterfactual, ownership boundary and economic mechanism before choosing a model. Reconcile revenue, margin, capex, taxes, working capital and financing definitions. Distinguish group cash-flow measures from incremental project FCFF and distinguish a limited discounted cash-flow window from full project NPV.

Use sensitivities and break-even thresholds when probabilities or commercial assumptions cannot be calibrated. Do not count protected profit and enabled growth twice, apply a separate sustainability premium to benefits already in cash flows, or convert an ESG score mechanically into WACC. A technical or environmental improvement does not establish investment attractiveness.

## Information dates and research status

Each sample identifies preparation and evidence dates. Historical research is not a live recommendation. Later methodological material must not be represented as available to an earlier investment decision. Post-publication observations and historical reconstruction are different records.

Use clear labels: **real-issuer research**, **historical case**, **illustrative model**, **synthetic demonstration**, **methodology-only study** and **proposed engagement**. These labels describe scope; none grants workflow approval or authority to invest.

## AI assistance and human accountability

AI may assist retrieval, drafting, structuring, implementation and testing. The October 2026 portfolio updates and research cases were prepared with AI assistance under Ed's brief. Their records do not claim independent human verification, issuer engagement or research approval where none is recorded.

The analyst's substantive contribution should be inspectable in the investment question, source challenge, counterfactual, assumptions, contrary case and decision criteria. Do not equate generated prose with analyst validation. Before investment use, a responsible human must review the evidence, model and material unresolved questions.

Most included engines validate and record structured artifacts; they do not independently retrieve every source, establish that a citation supports a claim, authenticate reviewers or execute trades. Some controls are programmed and others are procedural. Inspect each repository's claim-to-control documentation.

## Data handling and public publication

Use only authorized evidence. Do not place confidential employer, client or issuer information, personal data, credentials or unlicensed proprietary documents in a public repository. Do not submit confidential material to a model or service without the required permission and appropriate data-handling arrangements.

Publish original models, bounded analysis and source references rather than third-party report PDFs, page images or proprietary datasets. Attribution is not permission to redistribute. Referenced institutions do not endorse this portfolio.

These are handling instructions, not claims that the repository provides encryption, access control, automatic redaction or a secure document store. Host-specific privacy, retention and model-version records must be established for each actual research run.

## Evaluation and failure reporting

Separate arithmetic tests, schema/metadata checks, LLM extraction evaluation, human research review and investment-performance testing. The original Microsoft release had 13 case-specific unit tests and 48 engineered metadata cases; later model tests extend those software checks. They establish selected deterministic behavior. The separate October 8 source-extraction comparison archives actual assistant-session outputs and descriptive error counts, without establishing empirical avoided losses, productivity improvement or investment alpha.

An empirical comparison should retain frozen passages, prompts, label provenance, baseline outputs, disagreements and abstentions. Record review time only when actually measured. The October 8 labels were prepared and reviewed by separate assistant sessions; eight canonical fiscal/calendar-period labels differed. Both reference sets and their scoring sensitivity remain visible; independent human adjudication was not performed. Exact model revision and human review time are unavailable. Selection was prospective to the two prompts, not evidence of exclusion from model training. Publish observed failures and corrections; do not invent accuracy or time-saving statistics. A green CI badge is not scientific, commercial or legal validation.

## Stewardship

A proposed request is not an actual engagement. Receipt is not completion; a meeting is not an outcome; a policy statement is not proof of operating change. Attribute only the contribution supported by evidence. Missing public detail does not establish misconduct.

The Microsoft milestone ledger begins with no sent requests, issuer responses, agreed deadlines or observed outcomes. Any later status requires dated evidence and review rationale. No automatic email, vote, divestment or investment action follows from a status label.
