## What the model controls prove—and do not prove

Software tests can establish selected mathematical invariants: timing conventions, capital boundary consistency, revenue vs gross-profit accounting, physical energy conservation, conversion between sulfur and SO2, Tg/tonnes and GWh/MWh, and prevention of some double counting. These tests do **not** verify proprietary report statements, peer-reviewed climate-model efficacy, year-round uptime, manufacturer performance, contracts, plant-level engineering, future regulation, current prices or investment returns.

A hypothetical annual direct-cost floor is not a full social-welfare calculation. Solar-radiation modification does **not** remove CO2 and must not be substituted for carbon-reduction investment or priced as a verified carbon credit. Ship and power-equipment expenditures overlap under some formulations, requiring non-additive revenue bridges.

## Human and evidence gates before promotion

- Verify original issuer disclosures, contracts/quotes, statutory rules and independent primary scientific sources with exact publication dates, corporate and asset boundaries, units and licences.
- Build a realistic 8760-hour power trace and N-1/forced-outage treatment, preserving battery energy reserve, degradation and cash-flow ownership.
- Distinguish actual orders, qualified supplier capacity, product shipments, service commencement and free cash flow for manufacturers; commissioned versus leased MW, timing, AFFO, financing, dilution and development capex for data-centre REITs.
- Introduce consensus and market valuation **only** from synchronized, verified inputs. Freeze prospective probabilities/thresholds before outcomes occur, or label them unscored.
- Require independent scientific and legal/governance assessment before attributing any SRM adoption, environmental benefits or commercial supplier revenue.
- Retain draft PRs and older October publications separately; only merge after actual checks and accountable human review.

The work is maintained as **research workflow development** rather than actual trade authority, human-certified underwriting, client recommendation or demonstrated alpha.
