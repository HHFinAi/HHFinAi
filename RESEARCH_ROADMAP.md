## Next research gates — not yet completed

| Workstream | Required evidence or completion standard |
|---|---|
| Independent case review | A documented human review of sources, assumptions and material objections, separate from software-test results |
| Empirical extraction evaluation | A held-out set of public passages, independently reviewed labels where feasible, a comparable baseline and observed error/review-time results |
| Microsoft financial and asset refresh | Update the dated financial baseline and verify current deployment status; obtain site- or design-specific economics before claiming calibrated returns |
| Adaptation comparison | Compare an enabler such as Quanta with an adapter such as TSMC using current primary evidence, avoiding unsupported adaptation-revenue or water-loss estimates |
| Follow-up research note | Preserve the original evidence cutoff and show exactly which new evidence changes assumptions or judgment |

A proposed issuer is a research candidate, not a security recommendation. The Heidelberg CCS case is mitigation/transition economics; it is not relabelled as physical adaptation.

## Deliberately deferred

A large adaptation-company dataset, additional standalone agents, a complex dashboard and broad jurisdictional rule coverage are deferred until the core cases and review evidence are stronger. A small, defensible public-evidence dataset is preferable to an unverified reproduction of a bank universe.
