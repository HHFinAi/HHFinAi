## What to inspect

**Investment judgment:** read the conclusion, the counterfactual and the strongest contrary case. Ask which evidence would change the decision.

**Financial discipline:** inspect the model inputs, units, cash-flow boundaries, sensitivities and break-even conditions. Reported facts, management expectations and analyst assumptions are not interchangeable.

**Research execution:** follow the sources and run the deterministic calculations. The [evaluation record](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/blob/main/examples/microsoft-ai/EVALUATION.md) distinguishes arithmetic and metadata checks from the [observed source-extraction comparison](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/tree/main/evaluation/claim-accuracy). Inspect the frozen questions, prompts, raw responses, labels and errors before interpreting the results. Human adjudication, productivity gains and investment alpha are not established by this comparison.

**Prospective discipline:** the [21 October forecast register](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/tree/main/evaluation/prospective-research) freezes three narrow operating hypotheses, subjective probabilities and scoring rules before the selected Q3 releases. Outcomes are pending. It is separate from extraction evaluation and investment performance.

**Host integration:** the [recorded filesystem-host session](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/tree/main/examples/host-integration) exercises real research-mode submissions, stale-revision rejection, downstream invalidation and export. The reproducibility helper replays archived artifacts; it does not generate new research or certify human approval.

## Research approach and contribution

**Evidence → economic mechanism → financial model → investment judgment → stewardship or monitoring question.**

The original HHFinAi work is the implementation: counterfactuals, financial bridges, evidence boundaries, review controls and research-to-engagement handoffs. Third-party frameworks are attributed rather than presented as original inventions. AI assistance is disclosed; publication is not a claim that every source or assumption has received independent human validation.

## Responsible use of AI

AI supports research; people remain accountable for evidence, assumptions and decisions. Verify primary sources, disclose material AI assistance, protect confidential information and retain uncertainty. These workflows do not authorize autonomous trading or certify sustainability or regulatory compliance.

[Read the responsible AI policy](https://github.com/HHFinAi/HHFinAi/blob/main/RESPONSIBLE_AI.md) — practical standards for evidence, disclosure, privacy, human oversight and corrections.

## Use and limitations

Independent research and educational work, not investment advice, regulatory certification, an audited performance record or an autonomous trading service. The local engines organize and validate research; a human or separately authorized AI host retrieves evidence and performs substantive analysis. Cases and demonstrations carry their own dates and limitations. Material unknowns remain visible.
